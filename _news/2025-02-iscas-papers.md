Six papers co-authored with colleagues from TU Dresden and beyond were presented at the
**2025 IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS)**, covering topics from
memristive CNN cell topologies and threshold switch neuron circuits to PCM-based in-memory
computing and a live 4×4 M-CNN edge-detection demonstration.
